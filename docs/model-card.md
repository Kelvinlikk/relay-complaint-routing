# Model card: the product-routing classifier

**Use:** decision support for a financial-services intake team with multiple clients, per-destination human reviewers, and a known operational route for unclassified cases. The system predicts the product selected on a CFPB form; it does not adjudicate complaint validity, urgency, compliance, liability, or the remedy.

**Output:** scores for six specialist desks and a seventh “Other / review” label. The frozen June rule makes a single-desk recommendation only for qualifying card and mortgage cases; others remain with staff. The proposed pilot keeps every real transfer human-confirmed. “Other / review” has a training target and is never an automatic destination. A held case stays in intake; all escalation and deadline handling remains active. Human adjudication and the handling clock are upstream processes, not features.

**Models compared:** a class prior, TF–IDF logistic regression, categorical gradient boosting, fine-tuned two-layer BERT, and combinations. The classifier underlying the June rules blends 80% calibrated TF-IDF probabilities with 20% tabular CatBoost probabilities, then calibrates the mixture. BERT was a measured challenger and has zero weight in this routing policy. Published model scores and routing decisions are different metrics: macro-F1 uses every complaint; error among automated routes uses a selected denominator. A classifier can improve while the decision policy still fails.

**Allowed inputs:** text, state and a client identified from a mailbox or authenticated intake source before routing. CFPB's Company column is the eventual recipient. Retrospective use is an optimistic stand-in; do not look it up after routing, impute it from labels, or simulate it by copying a downstream field. An unseen institution differs from an unidentified one. A missing company triggers the demonstrated review policy rather than silent imputation. Model output is sensitive to institutional and product mix.

**Sample:** published narratives in selected dated archives, at least 10 words, with repeated text screened. Texts under ten words, missing texts and unpublished texts are excluded from the offline performance denominator; that denominator is not an institution's entire intake volume. The proposed 50-word safeguard is a later, exploratory rule; it does not change the original test denominator. Product choices can be ambiguous, labels may change, near-duplicate matching can miss paraphrases, and multi-product cases cannot have multiple gold destinations here.

**Operational gap:** no live bank pilot, no adjudicated local labels, no measured cost per error, no proof of fairness or present-day calibration. Use the recorded label only for the exercise. Locally, sample accepted and held cases; check resolution delays, transfers, queue coverage and handling time against adjudications. Run inference offline on approved text; this repository includes aggregate outputs and synthetic UI examples, not the underlying complaint narratives.
