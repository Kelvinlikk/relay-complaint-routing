# Field protocol: test the decision with real reviewers
**Sponsor:** head of complaint operations. **Current authorization requested:** a shadow trial. Staff make every real routing decision, preserving normal deadlines and independent urgency, vulnerability and language procedures. A public product classification is not an adjudicated local desk label.

### Track 1 • Would the handoff be reliable in this inbox?
Freeze the two thresholds and the proposed short-text hold. Before scoring, staff confirm the client independently, check scope and escalate as usual. Keep aggregate counts for all arrivals, then log every consecutive eligible arrival, including proposed holds and changes in language, channel and client mix. Report coverage among all arrivals separately from coverage among eligible cases. Take one candidate per known duplicate cluster; report calendar- and client-cluster sensitivity. Keep case keys pseudonymous; store sensitive records only in the operator’s approved system.

For **each** candidate destination, collect 400 consecutive, locally adjudicated recommendations that would pass the frozen policy. Adjudicators must be blind to the model's choice; have two specialists resolve disagreements under a pre-agreed taxonomy. At one fixed look, **at most 11/400** may be wrong: the one-sided 97.5% exact upper bound is 4.87%; 12/400 fails. Bonferroni control across two desks keeps the family error budget at 5% under the stated assumptions. If the true error is 2%, a single desk has an 89% chance of clearing this gate; if it is 3%, about 46%. A result of 11 is not a target. If cases cluster, the queue mix changes, there are too few unique cases, or severe delay is observed, keep the queue in review and investigate. Do not repeat the test until it passes. Any redesign requires a fresh, precommitted evaluation.

### Track 2 • Does showing two choices help a reviewer?
Enroll human-held eligible cases. Compare normal review with a screen displaying two suggestions plus an equally accessible “another desk” option. Staff may always discard both suggestions. Randomize cases in blocks within suggested desk and length band; balance assignments across trained reviewers and shifts before starting. Keep source text and ordinary routing aids identical across arms. Each case is seen once by a trial reviewer; an independent, blinded adjudicator supplies the reference. Include Other/out-of-scope as a valid decision, and log timeouts, absences, reconsiderations and unresolved labels. Never backfill them with the model's answer.

Record active seconds, the chosen desk, reference desk and critical escalation failures. Estimate the change in adjudicated accuracy and active time; report both arms and their denominators. Pair the contrasts across the two different cases in each randomized block; no individual case is reviewed twice. Report confidence intervals for these block-level contrasts. Inspect cases where both suggested desks were wrong for anchoring; assess first and final desk as distinct outcomes. No value is claimed from ranking until this comparison is run. Before enrollment, have operations specify the largest acceptable accuracy loss and the minimum useful time saving. Size the trial using run-in variance; support rollout only if the uncertainty intervals meet both prespecified criteria and serious escalation failures have been reviewed. The 400 per desk in Track 1 is **not** a sample-size calculation for Track 2.

The [local CLI](../experiments/transfer/pilot.py) generates blocked assignments, analyzes the paired trial and calculates the release gate. The input for assignment is a CSV with `case_key,suggested_desk,length_band` (`short` below 50 words). Use a randomly chosen recorded seed before any case outcome is seen. The output adds `pair_id,arm`; for the analysis append `decision,adjudicated_queue,active_seconds`. The primary matched contrast uses complete blocks and discloses singletons; arms also receive full descriptive summaries. Missing outcomes require an explicit missing-data sensitivity analysis. For repeated reviewers or clustered customers, use reviewer/cluster-aware inference as a sensitivity check; the simple block bootstrap is not a solution to those dependencies.

### Economics and monitoring
Only the automation scenario has a dollar estimate. Convert capacity into cash only if staff can use the recovered time; subtract actual implementation, training and audit costs. During a limited release, sample both routed and held cases; track calendar-day error, transfer delay and changed inputs. A critical misroute or broken client-input provenance immediately restores human review. Define scheduled re-evaluation with the oversight owner; the fixed-look calculation cannot be recycled for repeated daily release tests.
