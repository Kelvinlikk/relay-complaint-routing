# Sample-size extension: design fixed before scoring additional cases
The original model, frozen June policy, July outcomes and previously published May sample remain intact. No search over new cutoffs, models, months or seeds based on results.
1. Expand the May 2026 retrospective from the original 6,000-case draw to all 17,488 narratives with at least ten words. Retain the original subsample first and verify its ID fingerprint exactly. Screen the remainder against original history, June, July and retained May cases. Report aggregate coverage and product disagreement among would-be routes using the original June gates, plus ranked suggestions for the length-guarded review group. Keep original and additional observations disjoint. This is a backward stress test, not a second forward holdout.
2. Enlarge the TF-IDF logistic regression training set with the next 36,000 label-blind hashes after the original 26,000-case draw from May 2018–December 2019; reject duplicate IDs and exact/near-text matches to the original cohort, eligible May/June/July/August narratives, and already retained additions. Preserve exactly the shipped model hyperparameters and refit vocabulary and coefficients on the enlarged training data. Do not refit the production blend, its thresholds or BERT. Assess whether data alone helps this baseline.
3. Add up to 2,000 label-blind candidates for each of the eight historical test months, September 2020–April 2021; apply the same overlap filters, including all retained training additions. Compare both text classifiers on these new cases, the original historical test, and the already-opened July cohort. New cases are sampled equally by month before screening; do not interpret the resulting mix as deployment prevalence.
4. Report macro-F1 and accuracy on seven labels, by-month scores and paired receipt-day bootstrap intervals for changes in macro-F1. For automatic routes report wrong/routed, coverage and Wilson intervals. Treat public product labels as proxies. Use SHA-256 normalized-text and seeded 64-permutation MinHash word-trigram LSH (8 bands of 8; exact candidate Jaccard at least 0.80); approximate candidate retrieval can miss matches. Samples and model files stay local; deliver aggregate outputs, hashes and runnable source.
